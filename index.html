Darshan Prabhu

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IITB) , where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Preethi Jyothi. Prior to this, I completed my Master’s (2020-23) at IITB under the guidance of Prof. Preethi Jyothi and Prof. Sriram Ganapathy, where I worked on developing robust ASR architectures for accented speech.

I also had the opportunity to intern at Sony Research (2023-24), where I explored leveraging speech foundation models for ASR. More recently, I was part of Team EMMA (End-to-end Multi-channel Multi-talker ASR), led by Lukáš Burget and Samuele Cornell, at JSALT 2025 in Czech Republic.
