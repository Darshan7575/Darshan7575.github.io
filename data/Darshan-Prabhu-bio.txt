Darshan is a second-year Ph.D. scholar at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, where he works on Automatic Speech Recognition under the guidance of Prof. Preethi Jyothi. He has publications in leading conferences such as Interspeech and EMNLP, where he has also served as a reviewer. Prior to his Ph.D., he completed his Master’s at IIT Bombay under the same advisor, with a thesis on improving ASR performance in the presence of accents; a work that earned him the Dr. George B. Fernandes Award for Excellence in Research.
